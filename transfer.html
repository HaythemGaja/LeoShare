LeoShare

browser → browser · no email · no Drive · no upload

Files go directly from this browser to the other device over an encrypted WebRTC connection. The signalling server only introduces the two browsers — it never sees your files. Keep this tab open until a live transfer finishes. Files you queue while the other side is offline wait on this device (not on a server) and send automatically when they come online.
